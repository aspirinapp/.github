<h3 align="center">Even aspirin isn't that simple.</h3>

<p align="center">AI for safe medicine use at home.</p>

---

**aspirin.app** keeps track of every medicine in your home. It shows what you have and what has expired, spots the same active ingredient in two medicines and explains each leaflet in plain words, in your language.

The app is designed for the whole EU and will launch in English, German, Spanish, French and Ukrainian. It is in development: the features described here are plans, and it does not replace advice from a doctor or pharmacist.

## What the AI does

- **Recognises the pack.** It reads the box: name, strength, form and expiry date. The code on the pack (GS1 DataMatrix) gives the exact product, batch and expiry date.
- **Explains in plain words.** It drafts short leaflet summaries and translations. A pharmacist approves them before anyone sees them.

## What the AI does not do

- It never makes up doses, ingredients or interactions. Medical facts come from official medicine registers and licensed data, checked by fixed rules.
- It never says a medicine is "safe to take". It does not approve, prescribe or decide anything.
- It does not guess. If it isn't sure what it scanned, it asks you to compare with the box.

**People decide.** The app informs. You and your pharmacist or doctor make the decisions. Read more in [responsible-ai](https://github.com/aspirinapp/responsible-ai).
