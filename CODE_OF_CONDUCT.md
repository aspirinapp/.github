# Code of conduct

aspirin.app is about safe medicine use, so we keep our spaces respectful, careful and safe for everyone.

## Be respectful

- Be kind and patient. People come here with different backgrounds, languages and levels of experience.
- Criticise ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.

## Keep personal and medical data out

- Never post real personal data or real medical information in issues, pull requests, discussions or files: no names of patients, no health records, no photos of real prescriptions or medicine boxes with personal details.
- Use made-up examples instead.

## This is not a place for medical advice

Nothing in these repositories is a diagnosis, treatment or prescription. Please ask a doctor or pharmacist about your own medicines.

**In an emergency, call 112** (the EU emergency number) or your local emergency number.

## Reporting a problem

If someone breaks these rules, email **[info@aspirin.app](mailto:info@aspirin.app)** with the subject **Conduct**. A person on our team reads every message and replies within two working days. We treat reports confidentially.

We may remove content or block people who break these rules.
