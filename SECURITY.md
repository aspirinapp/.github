# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

If you find a security problem in anything published by aspirin.app, please email **[info@aspirin.app](mailto:info@aspirin.app)** with the subject **Security**.

Please include:

- what is affected (repository, file or page)
- how to reproduce the problem
- what impact you think it has

**Do not report vulnerabilities in public issues, pull requests or discussions.** Please give us a reasonable time to fix the problem before you share it anywhere else.

Do not include real personal or medical data in your report.

## What happens next

- A person on our team reads every report.
- We reply within two working days to confirm that we received it.
- We keep you informed while we look into it and tell you when it is fixed.
